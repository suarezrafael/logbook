# External outreach sequence for V81 and V87

## Objective

The present bottleneck is independent validation, not additional internal laboratory production. Outreach must ask narrow technical questions, avoid claims about the wider P-versus-NP program, and create a dated evidence trail.

## Sequence

### Stage 1 — public technical question

Post the V81 identity and balanced-cut consequence as a self-contained research-level question. The preferred first venue is Theoretical Computer Science Stack Exchange; MathOverflow is a secondary option if the framing is made purely combinatorial.

The question must ask for one or more of:

- a standard name for the identity;
- a literature reference that subsumes it;
- a correction to the active-vertex convention, balanced-tree lemma, or integer rounding;
- a stronger rank-three, simple, or linear-hypergraph consequence.

It must not ask whether the whole project is correct or whether the result is "novel" in the abstract.

A public permalink is valid evidence for the V81 external-submission gate. A local draft is not.

### Stage 2 — preprint preparation

Prepare a compact technical note with only externally defensible statements, definitions, proofs, scope, prior-art search, and reproducibility pointers. Do not upload merely to satisfy the gate. Submission requires authorship, license, category, abstract, bibliography, and endorsement checks to be complete.

The preprint must not mention the 47-laboratory chronology in its title or abstract. It may state the originating computational motivation only after the structural lemmas are presented.

### Stage 3 — Brazilian academic contact

Approach a theory-of-computation, graph algorithms, combinatorics, or discrete mathematics researcher with one packet and one question. An in-person or video conversation is preferred where feasible. The request is for identification of prior art or a proof defect, not supervision of the entire program.

A documented independent request concerning the V87 packet is valid evidence for the V87 external-submission gate.

### Stage 4 — targeted author email

Email an author only after identifying a specific paper, theorem, convention, or constant that plausibly overlaps the claim. Include a stable preprint or public technical note when available.

The email must have the form:

```text
Does theorem X under convention Y cover the following rank-three case Z?
```

It must not have the form:

```text
Could you review my P versus NP project?
```

## Gate accounting

| Item | Earliest qualifying evidence | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| V81 | permalink to a public, self-contained technical question or documented request to an independent expert | not submitted |
| V87 | documented request to an independent expert, public technical question, or targeted author email tied to a specific theorem | not submitted |

Posting, submission, or email must not be recorded until it has actually occurred. Silence is not validation. Any answer, correction, counterexample, citation, or refusal must be logged.

## Tone firewall

Do not use "P versus NP", "breakthrough", "solution", "47 laboratories", or claims of novelty in the title, subject line, or opening paragraph. Lead with definitions, one lemma, and one precise question.
