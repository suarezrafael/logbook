\begin{definition}[Pair transport]
Let
\[
 g_e(x_u,x_v,x_w)=\operatorname{MAJ}(x_u\oplus p_u,
 x_v\oplus p_v,x_w\oplus p_w).
\]
For a chosen pair $(u,v)$ define
\[
 \delta_e=1\oplus p_u\oplus p_v.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Exact pair implication]
For either $a\in\{0,1\}$ there is an output target $t_e$ such that the exact
constraint $g_e=t_e$ contains
\[
 x_u=a\Longrightarrow x_v=a\oplus\delta_e
\]
and its contrapositive.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose $t_e=1\oplus p_u\oplus a$.  A ternary majority target is exactly the
conjunction of the three binary clauses forbidding two local literals from
simultaneously disagreeing with the target.  The $(u,v)$ clause gives the stated
implication and its contrapositive.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Path transport]
For an oriented path $P=e_1\cdots e_q$ in a graph using one chosen pair per
output, targets can be chosen so that
\[
 x_s=a\Longrightarrow x_t=a\oplus\bigoplus_{e\in P}\delta_e.
\]
The same path clauses give the reverse contrapositive.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the exact pair implication successively, choosing each target for the
currently propagated source value.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Odd signed barbell]
A simple bicyclic component of the chosen-pair graph is an \emph{odd signed
barbell} if its $2$-core is either
\begin{enumerate}
\item two edge-disjoint cycles sharing one vertex (a figure-eight), or
\item two vertex-disjoint cycles joined by a path,
\end{enumerate}
and the xor of the transport labels around each of the two cycles is one.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Signed-bicyclic barbell avoider]
Let $C:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}^m$, $m>n$, have signed-majority outputs and use the
first two support variables of each output as its canonical pair.  If one
connected simple bicyclic canonical-pair component is an odd signed barbell,
then a word outside $\operatorname{Range}(C)$ is constructible deterministically
in polynomial time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Leaf pruning preserves the cycle rank.  In a connected simple bicyclic graph the
barbell/figure-eight core can be recognized from the degree pattern and traced
as two edge-disjoint cycles plus a possibly empty connector.

Choose a base literal $u:a$ on the first odd cycle.  By path transport around
that cycle,
\[
 u:a\Longrightarrow u:\neg a.
\]
If the cycles are vertex-disjoint, transport $u:\neg a$ along the connector to
$v:b$.  The same connector clauses give the reverse contrapositive
$v:\neg b\Rightarrow u:a$.  The second odd cycle gives
$v:b\Rightarrow v:\neg b$.  Hence
\[
 u:a\to u:\neg a\to v:b\to v:\neg b\to u:a.
\]
If the cycles share one vertex, the connector has length zero and the two odd
cycles directly give the two opposite implications at that shared variable.
In either case a variable and its negation lie in one SCC of the exact target
2-SAT implication graph, so the selected target constraints are inconsistent.
Complete all unselected output coordinates arbitrarily.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strict exact-stretch separation]
For every $r\ge2$ there is a switching-unbalanced signed-majority circuit with
\[
 n=r+6,\qquad m=n+1,
\]
whose canonical pair graph is an odd barbell and for which
\[
 \lambda=n,\qquad \mu=n,\qquad
 \beta=\left\lfloor\frac{2n}{3}\right\rfloor,\qquad
 \nu=n,\qquad \eta_{AF}=n.
\]
No proper output subset has positive surplus, while the V105 barbell avoider is
polynomial time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use two triangles joined by a path with $r$ internal vertices and give every
canonical pair transport label one.  Realize each pair edge by an essential
signed-majority gate.  Negate only the third literal of one left-triangle gate;
this leaves canonical pair transport unchanged but contradicts the switching
equations forced by the two positive gates on the same three inputs, so the
component is not switching-balanced.

The pair graph has $n+1$ edges on $n$ vertices and every proper edge subset has
at most as many edges as its pair vertices; circuit support is at least that pair
vertex set, so no proper positive-surplus output subset occurs.  Essential
ternary support, connectivity and minimum incidence degree at least two give
$\lambda=n$.  Signed majority has no exact functional target fiber and both
canonical target fibers have full affine hull, yielding $\mu=\nu=\eta_{AF}=n$.

For V102 let $U$ be the complement of a strong affine backdoor.  Every majority
support contains at most one vertex of $U$.  Consecutive path supports force a
distance-three packing, while each end triangle contributes at most one
compatible free vertex.  The maximum is $\lceil n/3\rceil$, attained by a
periodic distance-three packing.  Therefore
\[
 \beta=n-\lceil n/3\rceil=\lfloor2n/3\rfloor.
\]
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Prior-art boundary.}
The support shapes underlying barbell/figure-eight witnesses have direct
2GraphSAT forbidden-topological-minor prior art.  The laboratory claim is only
the signed-majority target-compatibility specialization and its range-avoidance
construction; novelty is not established.

\paragraph{Nonclaims.}
No algorithm for arbitrary signed-majority exact-stretch circuits, no
unrestricted $NC^0_3$ avoidance algorithm, no new circuit lower bound, no
novelty or peer-review claim, and no P-versus-NP resolution is asserted.
