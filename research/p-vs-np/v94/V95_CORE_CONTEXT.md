# V95 core context — canonical-prefix escape or comparison-free avoidance

## Starting point

V94 proves that exact child comparison on **arbitrary supplied prefixes** is
PP-complete for one explicit fixed arity-at-most-three gate language at exact
stretch one. The same compiler is explicitly noncanonical at its first `DEF`
bit, so no canonical-prefix hardness follows.

The affine control is polynomial because prefix fibers stay linear systems.

## Mandatory separation

V95 must not restate arbitrary-prefix PP-hardness as a barrier for range
avoidance. It must attack one of the two surviving objects directly:

1. the prefix sequence generated by the V92 minority/tie-zero policy; or
2. an avoided-output constructor that does not need exact child comparisons.

## Track A — canonicalization pressure

Try to transform the V94 selector compiler so that every prefix constraint
required by the counting reduction is also the bit selected by the V92 rule.
For every attempted gadget record the local child imbalance needed to force its
constraint bit and prove whether later gadgets preserve that imbalance.

Promotion target:

- a polynomial reduction proving hardness for **canonical** child decisions; or
- a theorem showing a structural invariant that prevents the V94-style
  selector reduction from being canonical for a broad, precisely defined
  gadget class.

Finite gadget search alone is not promotion.

## Track B — comparison-free construction

Search for a deterministic rule that only needs one-sided information such as
zero detection, coarse multiplicative estimates, modular information, or a
small target list, but still guarantees an absent output at `m=n+1`.

A claimed speedup must be end-to-end and compared explicitly against the
Huang-Li-Zhong `O(n*2^(n/2))` baseline for `k=3`.

## First concrete lemma target

Characterize when a required prefix bit `b` can be made canonical by adjoining
a constant-size local forcing gadget whose auxiliary variables have unique
extensions and whose count ratio survives composition. Either obtain a
composition theorem or an obstruction theorem.

## Stop rule

Do not spend another full laboratory enumerating small gadgets unless the
search yields a symbolic invariant, a composable forcing lemma, or an explicit
canonical reduction. Otherwise switch to comparison-free avoidance.
