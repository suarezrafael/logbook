# Core context for Laboratory V55

## Stable facts after V54

1. The V53 union-free substitution lemma is valid.
2. The V53 girth implication and logarithmic syndrome family are retracted.
3. Every `k`-uniform support hypergraph with `m>n` has a nonempty 2-core.
4. Pure `AND_k` therefore has an explicit target with separator degree at most `k+1` over every field.
5. Pure `AND3` has a quartic Range-Avoidance certificate.
6. UF2 has exact separator degree 3; UF3 has exact separator degree 4.
7. The singleton-fiber NPN orbit admits a signed forcing-core theorem.
8. Coherent polarities work at `m>n`; arbitrary polarities work when outputs exceed used signed literals, in particular `m>2n`.
9. Separator degree is standard point-separator / degree-d closure language.
10. General stretch-one `NC0_3-Avoid` remains open.

## Mandatory focus

Move beyond singleton fibers.

1. Exhaust all 14 ternary NPN classes and classify local forcing capabilities.
2. Define a finite-state forcing hypergraph whose vertices are partial assignments, not only signed literals.
3. Seek a core theorem for gates with fibers of size two or three.
4. Search for exact small circuits whose minimum separating degree exceeds four.
5. Combine loose-X-cycle/Turan structure with algebraic separators or hitting sets.
6. Study separator sparsity and circuit size, not only degree.
7. Contact GGNS or Kuntewar–Sarma only with the V53 correction stated explicitly.
