# Core context for Laboratory V57

## Stable facts after V56

1. The V53 girth implication and logarithmic syndrome family remain retracted.
2. V54's pure-AND and singleton-fiber results are valid, but the arbitrary-polarity threshold is strengthened by V56.
3. V55's block-subspace theorem is valid; V56 improves its general threshold from `m>n+1` to `m>n`.
4. Consistency-or-redundancy solves every mixture of efficiently represented affine output fibers at minimum positive stretch.
5. Both branches yield product separators of degree at most `n+1`.
6. Essential affine ternary NPN classes solved at stretch one: `0x01`, `0x06`, `0x18`, `0x69`.
7. Six essential non-affine classes remain: `0x07`, `0x16`, `0x17`, `0x19`, `0x1b`, `0x1e`.
8. Every fiber of every remaining class has minimum disjoint affine-partition number exactly two.
9. Both fibers are bijunctive for `0x07`, `0x17`, and `0x1b`.
10. `0x16`, `0x19`, and `0x1e` are not bijunctive on both fibers.
11. Projection of one affine extension is affine, so hidden affine selector variables cannot encode a non-affine fiber exactly.
12. General stretch-one `NC0_3-Avoid` remains open.
13. Novelty and prior art for the V56 formulation are not externally confirmed.
