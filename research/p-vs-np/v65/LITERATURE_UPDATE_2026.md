# Range-Avoidance literature update through 30 July 2026

This note records primary sources that materially affect the lower-bound interpretation of the repository.

## Sources

- Hanlin Ren, Rahul Santhanam, Zhikun Wang, *On the Range Avoidance Problem for Circuits*, ECCC TR22-048.
- Karthik Gajulapalli, Alexander Golovnev, Satyajeet Nagargoje, Sidhant Saraogi, *Range Avoidance for Constant-Depth Circuits: Hardness and Algorithms*, ECCC TR23-021 / APPROX-RANDOM 2023.
- Shengtang Huang, Xin Li, Yan Zhong, *Range Avoidance and Remote Point: New Algorithms and Hardness*, ECCC TR25-049, revision 5.
- Hanlin Ren, Yichuan Wang, Yan Zhong, *Hardness of Range Avoidance and Proof Complexity Generators from Demi-Bits*, ECCC TR25-191.
- Hanlin Ren, Ryan Williams, *Near-Maximum Circuit Lower Bounds for Exponential Time with Merlin-Arthur Queries*, ECCC TR26-118, revision 1.

## Interpretation

The 2025–2026 results strengthen both sides of the picture: Range Avoidance supports lower-bound frameworks, but it can also be hard under cryptographic assumptions and remains sensitive to circuit class, oracle allowance and stretch. Therefore the V56 affine theorem is best treated as a precisely delimited algorithmic island. Expanding that island to all ternary local circuits is a meaningful research objective, not a demonstrated path to P versus NP.

No source is interpreted as confirming novelty of V56, V57 or V58.
