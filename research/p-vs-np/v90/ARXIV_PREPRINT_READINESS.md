# Preprint readiness gate for the V81/V87 structural note

## Purpose

A preprint can provide a stable object for later academic outreach, but uploading an underdeveloped note merely to satisfy the external-validation hold would create a second internal artifact rather than independent validation.

## Minimum content

A submission candidate should contain:

1. a neutral title about hypergraph deficiency, connectivity boundaries, and rank-three width transfer;
2. an abstract stating only the two structural claims and their scope;
3. exact definitions of the branchwidth convention and active-vertex convention;
4. full proofs, including small and degenerate cases;
5. a literature section that searches for standard connectivity-function and hypergraph-width formulations;
6. finite verification described only as supporting evidence, not proof;
7. explicit nonclaims about novelty, algorithms, circuit lower bounds, and P versus NP;
8. reproducibility links to stable tagged repository material.

## Required checks before upload

| Check | Required state |
|---|---|
| authorship and contributor roles | explicitly confirmed |
| license | selected by the author |
| title and abstract | independent of the laboratory chronology |
| bibliography | primary sources checked |
| theorem conventions | reviewed against the literature |
| category choice | justified from the manuscript content |
| endorsement/account requirements | resolved by the submitting author |
| source archive | compiles from a clean checkout |
| public version | no private information or internal-only commentary |

## Suggested manuscript structure

```text
1. Introduction and literature question
2. Definitions
3. Deficiency–boundary conservation
4. Balanced branch-decomposition consequence
5. Rank-three primal-treewidth/branchwidth transfer
6. Degenerate cases and convention translations
7. Computational audits
8. Open questions and nonclaims
```

## Title candidates

- Deficiency Conservation Across Hypergraph Branch Cuts
- Two Structural Lemmas for Rank-Three Hypergraph Width
- Hyperedge Boundaries, Deficiency, and Primal Treewidth in Rank Three

## Stop condition

Do not upload while the literature boundary, authorship, license, or theorem conventions remain unresolved. A public technical question and direct academic conversation may precede the preprint and should be used to improve it.
