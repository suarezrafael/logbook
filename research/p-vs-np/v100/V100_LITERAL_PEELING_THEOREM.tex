\begin{definition}
An essential ternary gate $g$ has a \emph{literal-graph fiber} if for some
target $b$ its fiber is contained either in $x_v=a$ or in
$x_v=x_u\oplus c$ for distinct inputs $u,v$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Safe elimination]
If $g_e=b$ implies one of the literal relations above, delete output $e$ and
eliminate one input by the corresponding constant or copy/negation
substitution. Any word outside the residual range lifts, by inserting $b$, to a
word outside the original range.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every realization of the lifted target satisfies the chosen relation and hence
induces an input of the substituted residual circuit with the surviving target
word. The residual circuit deliberately forgets all other constraints of
$g_e=b$, so it is a relaxation; absence from the larger residual range implies
absence before relaxation.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Invariant]
Each elimination removes one input and one output, preserves $m-n$, and keeps
locality at most three.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Constant substitution cannot increase support. Replacing $x_v$ by
$x_u\oplus c$ only renames/complements that occurrence; a support of size at
most three remains of size at most three and shrinks when $u$ was already
present.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal-substitution preprocessing]
Arbitrary $NC^0_3$ range avoidance polynomially reduces, with a direct lifting
map and unchanged positive surplus, to an instance having no essential ternary
literal-graph fiber.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Repeatedly apply safe elimination to the first peelable ternary gate. Each step
removes one active input, so at most $n$ steps occur. Compose the lifting maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact ternary classification]
Among the $218$ essential ternary Boolean tables, exactly $144$ are
literal-graph peelable. They are the five NPN orbits
\[
0x01,\ 0x06,\ 0x07,\ 0x18,\ 0x19
\]
of sizes $16,24,48,8,48$. The residual NPN orbits are
\[
0x16,\ 0x17,\ 0x1b,\ 0x1e,\ 0x69
\]
of total size $74$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Literal-graph peelability is invariant under input permutation, input
complement, and output complement. Direct evaluation of one representative of
each of the ten essential ternary NPN orbits gives the stated list; the check is
on a fixed $256$-table universe.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Five-orbit polynomial class]
If every essential arity-three gate of the initial circuit belongs to one of
$0x01,0x06,0x07,0x18,0x19$ up to NPN equivalence, deterministic range avoidance
is polynomial time for every $m>n$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exhaustive literal peeling removes all essential ternary gates. A substitution
that leaves another gate ternary acts on it only by input rename/complement, so
its peelable NPN class is preserved; otherwise its arity drops. The residual
positive-surplus circuit is therefore $NC^0_2$. Apply the deterministic
polynomial-time $NC^0_2$ range-avoidance algorithm of
Guruswami--Lyu--Wang and lift the resulting missing word through the peel
records.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
The entire $48$-table $0x07$ NPN orbit left open after V99 is polynomial-time
avoidable at $m>n$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Strict non-unate pair-substitution family]
For every $N\ge5$ there is a connected exact-stretch, non-unate, essential
ternary family with V97 peeling parameter $\lambda=N$ that has no
constant-coordinate forcing fiber but is solved by literal substitution.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put the canonical $0x19$ gate on cyclic triples and duplicate one support. The
target-one fiber of $0x19$ forces equality of its first two inputs but fixes no
individual coordinate. Every input has support degree at least three, so V97
does not peel, while the literal-substitution theorem applies.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The reduction abstracts a pattern already present in the monotone gate
elimination of Kuntewar--Sarma and uses published $NC^0_2$ avoidance as its
base case. No novelty, unrestricted $NC^0_3$, lower-bound, or P-versus-NP claim
is made.
