\paragraph{Status.}
This module formalizes an internally verified construction from Laboratories V57--V64.
It does not claim novelty for general 2-CNF irredundancy, is not peer reviewed, and does
not imply a general algorithm or lower bound for $\mathrm{NC}^{0}_{3}$ range avoidance.

Let $x=(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3)\in\bits^4$ and define five blocks
\begin{align*}
B_1(x)&=\neg x_0\wedge(\neg x_1\vee x_2),&
B_2(x)&=\neg x_0\wedge(x_1\vee\neg x_2),\\
B_3(x)&=\neg x_0\wedge(\neg x_1\vee x_3),&
B_4(x)&=\neg x_0\wedge(x_1\vee\neg x_3),\\
B_5(x)&=\neg x_0\wedge(\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3).
\end{align*}
Write $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{5}B_i$.

\begin{theorem}[Complete block irredundancy]
The formula $F$ has the unique model $0000$. For every $i\in\{1,\ldots,5\}$,
there is an assignment satisfying all blocks except $B_i$ and falsifying $B_i$.
Consequently no complete block can be deleted without changing the model set.
After duplicate copies of $\neg x_0$ are collapsed, the resulting six-clause 2-CNF
is also clause-irredundant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The four binary clauses in $B_1,\ldots,B_4$ imply $x_1=x_2=x_3$ whenever all hold.
The last clause forbids $x_2=x_3=1$, hence $x_1=x_2=x_3=0$; the common unit clause
then gives $x_0=0$. Thus $0000$ is the unique model.

Block irredundancy is certified, in block order, by
\[
0101,\quad 0010,\quad 0110,\quad 0001,\quad 0111.
\]
Direct substitution shows that the $i$th assignment satisfies every $B_j$ with
$j\ne i$ and falsifies $B_i$.

After collapsing duplicates, order the clauses as
\[
\neg x_0,\ (\neg x_1\vee x_2),\ (x_1\vee\neg x_2),\
(\neg x_1\vee x_3),\ (x_1\vee\neg x_3),\ (\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3).
\]
Clause irredundancy is certified in this order by
\[
1000,\quad 0101,\quad 0010,\quad 0110,\quad 0001,\quad 0111.
\]
Each witness satisfies the other five clauses and falsifies the selected clause.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Orbit restriction]
Using the local variable orders in the machine-readable specification, the five block
truth-table masks are
\[
\mathtt{0x51},\mathtt{0x45},\mathtt{0x51},\mathtt{0x45},\mathtt{0x15}.
\]
All belong to the NPN orbit of $\mathtt{0x07}$, whose size is $48$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The accompanying verifier enumerates all $3!\,2^3\,2$ input permutations, input
negations and output negations, computes the orbit exactly, and checks membership of
the five masks.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Direct sums.}
For every $k\ge 0$, adjoining $k$ independent three-input/three-output bijective
components gives $n=4+3k$ and $m=5+3k=n+1$. A witness for deletion of an original
V57 block extends arbitrarily through the bijective components, so complete block
irredundancy of the distinguished component persists.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The normative data are in \texttt{V57\_BLOCK\_IRREDUNDANCY\_SPEC.json}.
The primary and independent Python verifiers exhaust all $16$ assignments and do not
rely on a SAT solver.
