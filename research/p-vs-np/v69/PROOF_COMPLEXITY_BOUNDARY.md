# Proof-complexity boundary for V69

`G*_proj` minimizes a repository-local projected affine residual DAG over gate orders. V69 proves no simulation to OBDD, FBDD, resolution, Res-Lin, or a communication matrix model.

Best-partition communication complexity and Tseitin/expander constructions are reasonable sources of experiments. Their lower bounds cannot be imported until a size-preserving translation proves that every small V69 DAG induces a small object in the target model, or conversely.

The order-optimization theorem is internal to the affine-cell model. It is not a circuit lower bound, an unrestricted `NC0_3-Avoid` algorithm, or a P-versus-NP consequence.
