# V117 literature boundary

## Source theorem used

Aleksandrs Slivkins, *Parameterized Tractability of Edge-Disjoint Paths on Directed Acyclic Graphs*, SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 24(1), 146–157 (2010), DOI `10.1137/070697781`.

The abstract states that DAG Edge-Disjoint Paths is W[1]-hard parameterized by the number of requests and that the hardness remains when the demand graph consists of two sets of parallel edges.

V117 uses exactly that restricted promise. It does not attribute the source hardness to the laboratory.

## Modern calibration

Ken-ichi Kawarabayashi, Nicola Lorenz, Marcelo Garlet Milani and Jacob Stegemann, *Directed Disjoint Paths Remains W[1]-Hard on Acyclic Digraphs Without Large Grid Minors*, IPEC 2025, DOI `10.4230/LIPIcs.IPEC.2025.2`, strengthens the modern hardness landscape for directed disjoint routing on DAGs.

V117 does not depend on the 2025 strengthening.

## New internal transfer

The only internally claimed step is the translation of the Slivkins two-parallel-demand hard family into the laboratory's capacity-one gate-resource language and its prescribed two-route feedback closure. Novelty of that exact formulation is not claimed without external review.
