# Candidate manuscript abstract

We study range avoidance for local Boolean circuits through the structure of output fibers. For circuits whose selected output fibers are efficiently represented affine systems, we give a deterministic polynomial-time consistency-or-redundancy algorithm at minimum positive stretch. The natural extension of this argument to bijunctive fibers fails: we exhibit five ternary local gates from a single NPN orbit whose selected 2-CNF fibers are jointly consistent yet completely irredundant, and extend the construction to an infinite stretch-one family. To quantify what remains after fixed-orientation redundancy fails, we define orientation depth, the Hamming distance from a chosen image point to the internal boundary, and obtain an `m^{O(d)} poly(n+m)` avoidance algorithm for bijunctive fibers. We also relate forcing to image-boundary edges, apply Boolean-cube isoperimetry to show global boundary abundance, and give an explicit family on which several natural strict-improvement potentials are flat. Finally, we separate deterministic localization from randomized avoidance: whenever image membership is polynomial-time and `m>n`, uniform output sampling is a Las Vegas algorithm using at most two expected membership tests. Our results are internally verified and accompanied by independent checkers; novelty of the affine formulation, the orbit-constrained bijunctive construction and orientation depth remains subject to external review.
